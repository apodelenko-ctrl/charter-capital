About AGI International

A Trusted Bridge
Between Producers
and Markets.

Founded in Montreal in 1998, AGI International Inc. has spent more than two decades connecting the world's best producers with the buyers who need them — restaurants, distributors, processors and retail chains across four continents.

We don't just move boxes. We manage every link of the chain — sourcing, specification, customs, cold-chain logistics and final delivery — so our customers receive exactly the product they ordered, on the day they need it.

  • 01
    Direct producer relationships

    Long-term partnerships with farms, processing plants and fisheries — vetted and audited by our team on the ground.

  • 02
    Specification-driven delivery

    Every shipment is built to the buyer's exact cut profile, packaging and labeling requirements — including halal, kosher and country-of-destination compliance.

  • 03
    Single point of accountability

    One account manager owns your shipment end-to-end. No handoffs, no excuses — your name and ours are on the bill of lading together.

Logistics

Cold chain.
Uninterrupted.

Frozen and perishable freight is unforgiving. One temperature breach in transit and the entire shipment loses commercial value. Our logistics team manages temperature, time and documentation with the same precision as the buyers we serve.

  • ❄
    Temperature-controlled, end-to-end

    −18 °C for frozen, 0–4 °C for chilled, monitored from origin loading bay to final delivery. Compliant with ISO 23412 and HACCP cold-chain standards.

  • ⚓
    Multimodal routing

    Sea, air, rail and road — combined to match the freshness window, volume and cost target of each shipment. Hundreds of reefer containers moved annually.

  • 📋
    Documentation & customs

    Country-of-destination health certificates, certificates of origin, halal & kosher attestations, and pre-cleared customs documentation prepared by our compliance desk.

  • 📡
    Real-time visibility

    Container-level GPS tracking and temperature telemetry. Proactive ETA management — you hear about a delay from us before you hear it from the port.

Quality Control

Inspected.
Documented.
Traceable.

Quality is the only thing that protects our customers from the cost of a rejected container. We audit our suppliers, we inspect our shipments, and we document everything — because in food trade, the burden of proof is on the seller.

  • ✓
    HACCP-compliant facilities

    Every supplier we work with operates under a validated HACCP plan and is regularly audited — by us or by third-party certification bodies.

  • ✓
    Lot-level traceability

    Every carton in every shipment is traceable back to the production lot, the processing facility and the date — within minutes, not days.

  • ✓
    Regulatory compliance

    CFIA, USDA, EU, GCC, MENA, and Asia-Pacific health authority requirements. Halal (HFSAA, IFANCA, ISA), kosher (OU, COR), and organic certifications available on request.

  • ✓
    Field-level QA

    Our quality team visits suppliers and customers throughout the year. We see the production lines and the receiving docks ourselves — not just on paper.
