Settlement rules

Rules Crypto Change

Dear clients using Charter Capital Crypto Exchange services. Please take note of the following rules.

This applies to the payment you send us in order to receive cryptocurrency. Settlement timing is calculated in Moscow time.

T+0 · same day

Payment before 12:00 MSK

Issued on the same day

To receive cryptocurrency on the same day, send the payment before 12:00 Moscow time.

Same-day window: before 12:00 MSK
T+1 · next day

Payment after 12:00 MSK

Issued the next day

Payments sent after 12:00 Moscow time are issued on the next business day: T+1.

After cut-off: T+1

Disclaimer

Charter Capital purchases the required volume of assets / cryptocurrency on wholesale OTC exchanges. Same-day release is therefore possible only if the payment is sent before 12:00 Moscow time: after that cut-off the volume is bought in the next session and issued T+1.

These rules apply to Charter Capital Crypto Exchange clients. Account details are reconfirmed before each payment.
