Project overview

A balanced unit-mix in a proven corridor.

The Title Artrio is a mid-scale residential condominium designed by Rhom Bho under The Title brand. The project addresses the standard demand pattern in Bang Tao — compact rental layouts (studios, 1-bed) with a smaller proportion of 2-bed units for owner stays.

Sale is structured as registered leasehold with a 30+30+30 renewal mechanism. Sale & Purchase, escrow flow and Land Office registration are reviewed by independent counsel.

Indicative net rental yield 6–8% p.a. in stabilised operation, conservative assumption against Bang Tao corridor benchmarks.
