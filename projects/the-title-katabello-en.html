Project overview

Phuket's classic holiday-rental territory.

The Title Katabello is a resort-grade condominium developed by Rhom Bho in cooperation with AssetWise in the Kata-Karon corridor. The territory is Phuket's most established holiday-rental zone — strong nightly-rate premiums in high season, slower shoulder seasons.

Sale is structured as registered leasehold with a 30+30+30 renewal mechanism. Independent legal review applies before signing.

Indicative net rental yield band 5–7% p.a. in stabilised operation. The corridor's seasonality means yield is rate-driven rather than occupancy-driven — high season carries the year.
