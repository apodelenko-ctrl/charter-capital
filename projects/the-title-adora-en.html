Project overview

Lifestyle condo with a documented rental programme.

The Title Adora is a lifestyle condominium in Bang Tao with an opt-in developer-managed rental programme. For selected layouts the programme includes indexed rental terms in THB, contractually documented as part of the unit structure.

Tenure is registered leasehold, 30+30+30 mechanism. The rental programme is a separate contract attached to the unit — independent counsel reviews both before signing.

Indicative net yield band 6–8% p.a. in stabilised operation. The indexed component is documented in the contract — not a brochure projection.
